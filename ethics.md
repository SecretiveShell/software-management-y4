# Ethics

## Public

The project has a negative ethical impact on customers by sacrificing human connection and the high specificity of technical support for a potenitally lower quality automated system. The customers might be exposed to additional privacy risks or find the system harder to access, so we have an ethical resposibility to ensure the accessibility and security of the final implementation.

There are some instances however where we can deliver on our ethical contract with more success, by providing faster turn around times for support inquiries for a more global audience.

## Employer (john jones)

During the rollout of this project, john jones will have an ethical obligation to his existing staff during the layoff process. Since layoffs can cause significant financial hardship and put staff at risk of poverty or destitution, loose health insurance benefits and other dependencies, special care must be taken to handle this responsibility in an ethical manner. It is important that John Jones will provide staff with a notice period and support seeking alternative employment, and that staff have financial support such as unemployment benefits and redundancy pay available if required.

Outside of the duty of care to employees, John Jones also has an ethical responsbility to customers to maintain and support the ongoing operations of the chatbot by ensuring the technical responses are accurate and up to date, that data is secure and that there is no discrimination.

If John Jones moves to a more international market, there is risk that the english only chatbot will cause indirect cultural discrimination though its inaccessibility to foreign speakers.

## Product

The project has an ethical obligation to ensure the product meets the acceptance criteria agreed for an acceptable cost. The chatbot should only answer the questions that it is capable of answering and not assume anything. The chatbot should ensure adequate: testing, debugging, documentation and review of software. When taking into consideration the future ethical impact of the software, we should ensure the chatbot is maintaniable.
