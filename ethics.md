# Ethics

## Public

The project has a negative ethical impact on customers by sacrificing human connection and the high specificity of technical support for a potenitally lower quality automated system. The customers might be exposed to additional privacy risks or find the system harder to access, so we have an ethical resposibility to ensure the accessibility and security of the final implementation.

There are some instances however where we can deliver on our ethical contract with more success, by providing faster turn around times for support inquiries for a more global audience.

## Employer (John Jones)

During the rollout of this project, John Jones will have an ethical obligation to his existing staff during the layoff process. Since layoffs can cause significant financial hardship and put staff at risk of poverty or destitution, loose health insurance benefits and other dependencies, special care must be taken to handle this responsibility in an ethical manner. It is important that John Jones will provide staff with a notice period and support seeking alternative employment, and that staff have financial support such as unemployment benefits and redundancy pay available if required.

Outside of the duty of care to employees, John Jones also has an ethical responsbility to customers to maintain and support the ongoing operations of the chatbot by ensuring the technical responses are accurate and up to date, that data is secure and that there is no discrimination.

If John Jones moves to a more international market, there is risk that the english only chatbot will cause indirect cultural discrimination though its inaccessibility to foreign speakers.

## Product

The project has an ethical obligation to ensure the product meets the acceptance criteria agreed for an acceptable cost. The chatbot should only answer the questions that it is capable of answering and not assume anything. The chatbot should ensure adequate: testing, debugging, documentation and review of software. When taking into consideration the future ethical impact of the software, we should ensure the chatbot is maintaniable.

## Judgement

All parties involved in the project have an ethical responsibility to support and maintain human values when making decisions. We should maintain professional objectivity and ensure that decisions are not influenced solely by the financial benefits of the project, but also take into consideration the impact on both customers and existing employees.

All parties should avoid deceptive financial practices, such as double billing or misrepresenting the costs and expected financial benefits of the project. It is also important that any conflicts of interest that can't reasonably be avoided are disclosed to all relevant parties.

When making professional judgements throughout the project, we should be honest about the capabilities and limitations of the chatbot and ensure that the decisions being made do not compromise the security, accessibility or quality of the final product for the purpose of reducing costs.

## Management - Jake

## Profession

Throughout the project, we have an ethical responsibility to create a working environment that encourages all members of the team to act ethically and support one another. We should ensure that decisions are made in the best interests of the project and its stakeholders rather than promoting our own personal interests.

We also have a responsibility to promote public knowledge of the software engineering profession by being transparent about the capabilities and limitations of the AI chatbot, ensuring that customers have a realistic understanding of the technology.

All members of the development team should take responsibility for identifying, correcting and reporting errors within the software. This is particularly important for the chatbot as incorrect technical advice could cause further problems for customers. Any significant violations of the Code of Ethics should be reported to the appropriate authorities rather than being ignored for the purpose of meeting deadlines or reducing costs.

## Colleagues - Jake

## Self

Throughout the project, we have an ethical responsibility to further our knowledge of recent developments in AI and relevant professional standards. We should improve our ability to develop safe, reliable and useful software whilst ensuring that the documentation is accurate and informative to support the future maintenance of the chatbot.

All members of the team should avoid influencing others to breach the Code of Ethics, regardless of the financial or time constraints of the project.
