## Business context and objectives

The client currently has 50 support staff and operates internationally, providing support 24 hours a day. Customers contact the business by telephone, with approximately 100–200 calls received each day.

The client expressed dissatisfaction with the current staffing arrangements and wants to reduce support costs and increase profitability through automation. Low cost, fast responses and high-quality support were identified as priorities.

The client mentioned a friend's use of a product recorded as “Wordpush”. The product name and its relevance to this project require confirmation.

## Proposed customer support system

The client wants an AI chatbot that can answer customer queries and provide useful advice. The desired experience is natural and human-like, maintaining the customer's sense that support is available. Whether this refers only to conversational style or also to how the system identifies itself requires clarification.

The system is expected to provide support 24 hours a day. Text, audio and video were mentioned as desired interaction formats, although the specific functionality and delivery phase for each format were not established.

Frequently received support queries include:

- Which filament can be used with a printer.
- A printer failing to power on.
- A printer failing to print correctly.

Fallback to a technician was identified as a requirement. The conditions for escalation and the process for transferring a customer to a technician remain to be defined.

## Intended delivery phases and staffing outcomes

### Phase one — Initial deployment

The client intends to deploy the automated support system and reduce support staffing from 50 to 10, representing an 80% reduction. The stated target is to have 10 support staff remaining by the end of the year.

The exact deployment date and the time available for a pilot and assessment before the staffing change were not recorded.

### Phase two — Full support replacement

After the initial system has been deployed, the client eventually intends to replace the remaining 10 support staff. This represents an eventual 100% reduction in support staffing.

No deadline was recorded for phase two. The role of technician fallback under this future operating model requires clarification, including whether technicians are separate from the support staff being replaced.
